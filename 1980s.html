1980 – 1989

Totally '80s

Neon. Spandex. Big hair. Let's go back in time!

The '80s Outfit Catalog

Denim & Neon Duo

Acid-wash jeans and neon jackets for the win!

Details

This look screams vintage cool with acid-wash denim, high-top sneakers, and neon pink shades. Iconic for a reason! The '80s neon trend, mixed with a classic denim vibe, made a bold statement of casual rebellion.

Work It Out Aerobics

Leotards, leggings, and headbands. Jane Fonda vibes!

Details

Get sweaty in style with a leotard, neon tights, leg warmers, and a headband. The aerobics craze of the '80s gave birth to a fitness-inspired fashion movement. Think Jane Fonda, or Olivia Newton-John in the "Physical" video!

Power Shoulders & Glam

Structured blazers and glitter all day.

Details

Shoulder pads, bold prints, and bright lipstick make this outfit a real showstopper. A quintessential power move, these outfits were popular with both men and women in business and pop culture.

Zebra Print Party Dress

Party hard with animal print and funky accessories.

Details

Layered skirts, animal print, plastic bangles: everything that screams '80s party mode! The bold zebra print and unconventional shapes made this a staple for partygoers and club lovers of the era.

Breakdance Streetwear

Tracksuits, sneakers, and boombox swagger.

Details

Snapback hats, Adidas tracksuits, and gold chains. Breakdance battles not included. This style was born on the streets and embodied the hip-hop culture that ruled the '80s.
